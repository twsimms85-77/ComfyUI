What Moves Cotton — driver cheat sheet

A quick reference for reading the news flow above.

Supply side

U.S. acreage & abandonment

West Texas grows roughly half the U.S. crop, much of it dryland. Drought there drives abandonment — the gap between planted and harvested acres — and can erase millions of bales between the March Prospective Plantings and final Ginnings numbers.

Weather windows

Planting (Apr–Jun) needs moisture; boll-setting (Jul–Aug) is hurt by extreme heat; harvest (Sep–Dec) is hurt by rain and early freeze. India's monsoon (Jun–Sep) and Xinjiang's growing season matter just as much for world supply.

India policy (MSP & exports)

India is a top producer. Its Minimum Support Price sets a floor under domestic prices, and CCI stockpiling or surprise export/import duty changes shift exportable surplus quickly.

China reserves & import quota

China's state reserve auctions and its sliding-scale import quota decide how much foreign cotton China pulls in. Reserve build = bullish import demand; reserve release = bearish.

Brazil's expansion

Brazil's safrinha cotton (Mato Grosso, planted after soybeans) has made it the world's top exporter in recent years — its harvest (Jun–Aug) and logistics set the tone for the export market.

Demand & money flow

Mill demand & export sales

Thursday's USDA Export Sales report is the weekly demand pulse — net upland sales and shipments vs. the pace needed to hit the WASDE export target. China, Vietnam, Pakistan, Turkey and Bangladesh are the buyers to watch.

Polyester substitution

Cotton competes with polyester at the fiber level. Cheap crude → cheap polyester → mills blend away from cotton. Watch crude/PTA prices as a slow-moving ceiling on cotton demand.

Macro & the dollar

Cotton is a consumer-discretionary fiber: apparel demand follows global growth. A strong dollar makes U.S. cotton dearer abroad and pressures exports.

Spec positioning

Managed-money extremes (chart at left) mark crowded trades. Heavy net-short + bullish surprise = short-covering rallies; record net-long + bearish surprise = liquidation breaks.

Certified stocks & basis

ICE certified stocks and the on-call sales/purchases report (unfixed mill buying) show physical tightness. Big unfixed on-call sales into a delivery month can fuel squeezes.
