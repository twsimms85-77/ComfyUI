# Coffee Supply News Sources & Historical Market Movers

A curated reference for tracking the coffee market (ICE Arabica "C" futures, ticker **KC**, quoted in US cents/lb; ICE Robusta futures, quoted in USD/tonne). Sources are tiered by how directly they move or explain price.

---

## Tier 1 — Official Supply & Demand Data (the reports that move futures)

| Source | What it publishes | Cadence | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| [USDA FAS — Coffee: World Markets and Trade](https://www.fas.usda.gov/data/coffee-world-markets-and-trade) | Global production, consumption, ending-stocks estimates | Semi-annual (June & December) + GAIN country annuals | The most-watched global balance sheet; a surprise Brazil or Vietnam number routinely gaps the market |
| [Conab (Brazil)](https://www.conab.gov.br/) | Official Brazilian crop surveys | ~4 surveys/year | Brazil is ~35–40% of world supply; Conab vs. USDA divergence is itself a trading story |
| [ICO — International Coffee Organization](https://www.ico.org/) | Monthly Coffee Market Report, Composite Indicator Price, export stats | Monthly | The industry's canonical demand/export dataset |
| [ICE Exchange reports](https://www.ice.com/products/15/Coffee-C-Futures) | Certified stocks, deliverable inventory | Daily | Certified-stock drawdowns preceded the 2024–25 record rally; traders watch this daily |
| [CFTC Commitments of Traders](https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/index.htm) | Managed-money (spec) positioning in KC | Weekly (Fri) | Extreme spec length/shortness flags squeeze and washout risk |
| [CEPEA/ESALQ (Univ. of São Paulo)](https://www.cepea.esalq.usp.br/en) | Brazilian physical (cash) arabica & robusta indicators | Daily | Physical-market confirmation of what futures are doing |
| Vietnam GSO / Customs export data | Monthly robusta export volumes | Monthly | Vietnam is the #1 robusta origin; export pace vs. expectations moves the London market |
| [FNC — Colombian Coffee Growers Federation](https://federaciondecafeteros.org/) | Colombian production & export stats | Monthly | Colombia is the benchmark washed-arabica origin |

## Tier 2 — Trade Houses & Analyst Research (crop estimates that lead official data)

- **[StoneX Coffee Insights](https://www.stonex.com/en/insights/)** — frequent, free market commentary; their Brazil crop estimates are widely cited ([example](https://www.stonex.com/en/insights/coffee-futures-shift-as-2026-supply-outlook-reshapes-the-market/)).
- **Volcafe (ED&F Man)** — their Brazil arabica deficit/surplus estimates have single-handedly moved the market (their 2024 deficit call helped fuel the record rally). Distributed via Reuters/Bloomberg wires.
- **Rabobank Agri Commodity Markets Research** — quarterly coffee outlooks, strong on the demand side.
- **Hedgepoint Global Markets** — weekly coffee reports, strong Brazil coverage.
- **Sucden Financial** — quarterly softs reports, good robusta coverage.
- **Marex** — softs positioning and volatility commentary.
- **[ING Think commodities](https://think.ing.com/)** — macro + softs outlooks ([example](https://think.ing.com/articles/brazil-crop-recovery-to-push-coffee-prices-lower/)).

## Tier 3 — Daily Market News & Commentary

- **Reuters Commodities** — the wire of record for coffee; Marcelo Teixeira's coverage of Brazil/NY softs is essential.
- **Bloomberg Commodities** — breaking supply news, positioning stories.
- **[Barchart coffee commentary](https://www.barchart.com/futures/quotes/kc*0/futures-prices)** — free daily "why coffee moved today" notes (Rich Asplund's column, syndicated to Nasdaq/Yahoo Finance).
- **[TradingEconomics — Coffee](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/coffee)** — free live-ish price, chart, and a running news ticker.
- **[Investing.com — Coffee Futures](https://www.investing.com/commodities/us-coffee-c)** — quotes plus aggregated headlines.

## Tier 4 — Industry Trade Press (supply chain, policy, origin stories)

- **[Daily Coffee News (Roast Magazine)](https://dailycoffeenews.com/)** — best US industry outlet; has RSS.
- **[Global Coffee Report](https://www.gcrmag.com/)** — trade/logistics/origin features.
- **[Comunicaffe International](https://www.comunicaffe.com/)** — daily industry newsletter, strong European/Italian roaster angle; has RSS.
- **[Perfect Daily Grind](https://perfectdailygrind.com/)** — market explainers and origin reporting; has RSS.
- **[Helena Coffee Vietnam](https://www.helenacoffee.vn/)** — Vietnam-side robusta market commentary ([example forecast](https://www.helenacoffee.vn/coffee-price-forecast-2026-2027-part-1/)).

## Tier 5 — Weather (historically the #1 price mover)

- **[NOAA Climate Prediction Center — ENSO](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso_advisory/)** — El Niño/La Niña advisories; ENSO phase drives both Brazil rain and Vietnam drought risk.
- **Somar Meteorologia / Climatempo / Rural Clima (Brazil)** — the frost and drought calls for Minas Gerais that traders act on (typically via wire-service coverage).
- **Brazil frost season: June–August.** Any cold-front story for Sul de Minas / Cerrado Mineiro during these months is a mandatory read.

## What Has Historically Moved the Coffee Market

1. **Brazilian frost** — the classic shock. Black frost of **July 1975** (destroyed ~70% of Brazil's trees, prices tripled), **1994's** double frost, and the **July 2021** frost that kicked off a rally from ~$1.50 to $2.60/lb.
2. **Drought in Brazil** — **2014** (Minas Gerais drought, prices ~doubled), **2024** (worst drought in decades hit the 2025/26 crop outlook and drove arabica to an **all-time high near $4.40/lb in Feb 2025**).
3. **Vietnam drought / robusta squeezes** — 2015–16 El Niño, and **2023–24**, when Vietnamese drought plus farmer withholding sent robusta to record highs and dragged arabica up with it.
4. **ENSO cycles (El Niño / La Niña)** — the macro weather driver behind most of the above.
5. **ICE certified stocks** — multi-decade-low certified inventories in 2023–24 removed the market's buffer and amplified every weather scare.
6. **Speculative positioning** — record managed-money length in 2024–25 amplified the rally; spec liquidation drives the violent corrections.
7. **Currency — BRL/USD** — a weak Brazilian real makes farmers eager sellers (bearish); a strong real slows origin selling (bullish).
8. **Logistics shocks** — the 2021 container shortage and 2024 Red Sea rerouting (which delayed Asian robusta to Europe) both tightened deliverable supply.
9. **Trade & regulatory policy** — the **EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR)** compliance scramble; **2025 US tariffs on Brazilian goods** disrupted flows into the US, the world's largest consumer.
10. **Biennial bearing cycle** — Brazil's arabica alternates on/off years, setting the baseline surplus/deficit expectation every season.
11. **Official report surprises** — USDA/Conab/Volcafe estimates that deviate from consensus gap the market on release day (e.g., USDA's June 2026 record 71.9M-bag Brazil forecast pressuring prices).

**The pattern:** coffee's biggest moves come from *supply* shocks at the two dominant origins (Brazil arabica, Vietnam robusta), transmitted through weather, and amplified by low certified stocks, spec positioning, and currency. Demand moves slowly; supply moves violently.

## Live Price Data Options (for apps)

| Option | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [TradingEconomics API](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/coffee) | Paid (free guest tier is limited) | Coffee spot/futures + calendar |
| [Commodities-API](https://www.commodities-api.com/) | Freemium | Arabica (COFFEE/KC00) rates; hourly on free tier, faster on paid |
| [Barchart OnDemand / Market Data APIs](https://www.barchart.com/ondemand) | Freemium | getQuote for KC futures; generous developer tier |
| Yahoo Finance (`KC=F`, `RC=F`) unofficial endpoints | Free | Delayed ~10–15 min; fine for a dashboard, not for trading |
| ICE direct market data | Expensive | The real-time source of truth; exchange fees apply |
| Alpha Vantage `COFFEE` endpoint | Free tier | Monthly/daily global coffee price (not tick data) |

**Practical default for a personal app:** delayed KC front-month via Yahoo/Barchart (15-min delay, free) + daily settlement history, clearly labeled "delayed."
