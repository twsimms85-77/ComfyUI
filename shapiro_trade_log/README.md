# Three Pillars Log

A single-file trade setup log built around Jason Shapiro's contrarian checklist.

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build, no server, no dependencies. Entries are saved in the browser's local storage. Use "Copy log as JSON" and "Paste JSON to import" to move entries between machines.

## The three pillars

1. **Positioning** — speculator positioning at an extreme in the weekly CFTC Commitments of Traders report.
2. **Sentiment** — consensus, surveys and headlines all leaning one way.
3. **Market Action** — the news failure: confirming news arrives and price moves the other way. Markets top on bullish news and bottom on bearish news.

A setup qualifies only when all three point in the same direction. Two of three is a watch, not a trade.

## Risk first

Every entry has an entry price, a stop, and a fixed dollar loss. The app computes stop distance and position size from those, so the maximum loss is known before entering.
